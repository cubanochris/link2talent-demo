Frequently asked questions

Can I customize how the receptionist handles calls? ▼
Yes. You can customize the greeting, the types of actions it takes (book, capture, transfer), and the tone of voice. Instructions are simple — no coding needed.
Does it work with my existing phone number? ▼
Yes. You point your existing number to us, and we handle the rest. No change to your business phone or any complex setup required.
What about missed calls or voicemail? ▼
The AI answers calls 24/7, so missed calls are rare. If a caller leaves a voicemail, it's transcribed and sent to you automatically.
How quickly can I get started? ▼
You can be live in under an hour. Set up your preferences, point your number to us, and you're ready. No long sales cycles, no engineering teams.
What if I want to transfer a call to a real person? ▼
You can configure transfer options. If the caller asks for a person, says something the AI isn't trained for, or if you want to jump in, transfers work smoothly.
Is my call data secure and private? ▼
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We're SOC 2 compliant and take privacy seriously. You control all retention and deletion policies.
